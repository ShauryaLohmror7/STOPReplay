SHARED ART DIRECTION
Near-black aubergine #0D0B14, pale lilac #DCD2F4, muted purple #A78BD8.
Light alternative: pale lavender #E6DDF2, paper lilac #F0EBF5, ink #14101F.
Code-style typography throughout. Use an easily readable monospaced face for conversations.
Use glass for the timeline thumb, a selected time plane, and limited floating controls.
Maintain opaque or nearly opaque backgrounds behind dense text.
The screenshots are art direction, not verified color-contrast specifications.

01 — PARTICLE ARCHIVE
Meaning: each slice is a recorded turn; highlighted particle groups correspond to evidence available by that turn.
Entry: previously recorded slices assemble in sequence over roughly 600–900 ms, then settle.
Scrub: rotate/translate the archive to intersect the selected slice with a stationary glass plane.
Selection: the corresponding transcript passage and source reference highlight together.
Disclosure: one short trail travels from the selected event to its disclosure row, once when crossing the event.
Rewind: future evidence disappears from the selected historical state; the historical event remains stored.
Hover: isolate an event group and reveal its time, type and evidence link.
Idle: very slight particle drift within bounds, preserving readable selection and exact event identity.
Best fit: the main time-machine screen and architecture explanation.

02 — GHOST TWIN
Meaning: the portrait identifies the simulated person; labels and attached evidence groups represent the known state.
Entry: ASCII portrait resolves once; evidence labels join from their source events.
Scrub: a narrow optical slice moves across the portrait while its evidence annotations update.
A small controlled change in point-cloud perspective can suggest time travel without moving the text.
Keep the portrait identity stable. Evidence visibility encodes time; face completeness is not a risk or psychological score.
Disclosure: its label lights once and a small particle group connects to the quoted message.
Click source: open the underlying message or record in an inspector; retain the current replay position.
Rewind: restore the earlier annotations, transcript, disclosure list and count together.
Best fit: the strongest single-screen identity for the pitch and the main replay experience.

03 — REPLAY CORRIDOR
Meaning: the time machine consists of the actual recorded conversation turns.
Entry: a restrained camera move enters the recorded thread.
Scrub: the selected sheet comes forward; previous sheets recede in depth.
When selected, the reading surface settles face-on. Full message content should be available in a stable flat reading pane.
Significant turns can carry supplied labels such as pushback, adaptation, correction and confirmed disclosure.
Those labels must come from reporting data, not a new model interpretation created during rendering.
Jump controls move directly to the next or previous turning point.
Best fit: an optional stage/demo presentation of the same replay, with full navigation still available.

04 — EXPOSURE ATLAS
Meaning: sourced observations, analysis and disclosed facts form a historically accurate map around the AI twin.
Entry: portrait resolves; nodes appear in recorded order, then stop moving.
Scrub: nodes and links restore to those available at the selected turn.
Hover: a single relevant evidence path is emphasized.
Select: the path opens the supporting source and focuses the corresponding transcript passage.
Use graph links only for actual evidence, revision or outcome relationships; proximity alone must not imply causation.
Best fit: profiles and evidence inspection, or an alternative lighter main workspace.

PROJECT-WIDE APPLICATION
Home: five modest stage indicators (Collect, Store, Analyze, Simulate, Replay), selected run, status and recent outcomes.
Transition into a run: the selected session's small portrait or particle mark expands into its replay view.
Logs: stable, readable chronological rows with channel, time, operation status and expandable detail.
Profiles: pixel portrait plus source-backed information and separate observation/inference status.
Time Machine: a single selected turn drives portrait/archive, transcript, findings, logs and metrics.
Attacks: scenario and per-run rows with timestamp, channel, outcome, run count and success denominator.
Opening an attack run moves to the matching recorded session; it does not execute the run.
Preserve navigation identity and typography across all five pages.

MOTION AND DATA RULES FOR IMPLEMENTATION
- User input updates the selected turn immediately. Motion follows selection rather than delaying data.
- Avoid animating metric interpolation through values unsupported by the selected turn.
- Give event-specific animations a brief duration, then settle for reading.
- Reduced-motion mode keeps the same data, selection and controls with stationary visualizations.
- Provide keyboard previous/next, play/pause and direct event selection.
- Live arrivals preserve the current inspected position until Follow live is selected.
- Scrubbing reads persisted history. It creates no new model calls or simulator actions.
- Public knowledge, inferred findings and confirmed disclosure remain distinct.
- Count unique confirmed disclosures separately from new disclosures this turn.
- Percentage examples in the concepts use a fictional known denominator: 3 of 10 tracked items.
  If the real denominator is unknown, show the count.
- Exact reporting fields and disclosure definitions remain to be agreed with Gary and Anushree;
  historical storage reads remain Cem's boundary. No shared fields or backend schemas were changed.
- Any platform implementation will need performance, keyboard, contrast and reduced-motion verification.
